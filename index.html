The setback

One bad week breaks the plan.

A work trip, a sick kid, a holiday week, a bad stretch of sleep. Rigid plans treat a missed week like a failure and send you back to week one. That's how people quit.

The coach reroutes around it, like a trail that bends around a washout, and keeps you moving toward the same goal.

The research

Too much research. No time to read it.

Tendon loading, polarized training, protein after 40, strides, sleep. All of it matters a little, and the studies don't agree on how much. You have a job.

The coach stacks the research, resolves it to your situation and tells you what's worth your effort. Sometimes the answer is skip it.

How it works

Your watch talks. The coach listens. You get a message.

  1. Tell it your why

    A short intake. Your goals, your history, your old injuries and what you want the next ten years to look like.

  2. Connect your watch

    Garmin and COROS connect through intervals.icu, a free training log. Apple Watch and others are being checked now. Runs flow in on their own.

  3. Get tomorrow tonight

    An evening message with the next session. A weekly message with the full plan.

  4. Push back anytime

    Ask why. Tell it you only have 30 minutes or your week blew up. It explains and adjusts.

  5. Hear it straight

    If something sounds like a warning sign, it tells you to stop and see a doctor or physical therapist. Same rule for everyone.

Questions

The short answers.

Does it replace my running watch?

It uses your watch. Garmin and COROS work today, and Apple Watch and other watches are being checked now. Your watch keeps tracking, and the coach turns that data into a plan fit to you.

Who is it for?

Runners 40 and up who already train and want to add strength without getting hurt. The first outside testers are men. The women's version of the training gets its own review by a woman clinician, and women join after that review. Beginners are welcome later with a simpler start.

What about injuries?

It's a training coach, not a medical provider. If something sounds like an injury, it tells you plainly to see a physical therapist or a doctor. If your clinician gives you a loading plan, the coach builds your training around that plan.

Do I need to download an app?

There's no fitness app to open. The coach messages you in Telegram, a free messaging app you may already have.

When can I start?

Testing starts small and grows slowly so it stays safe. Join the list and I'll let you know when there's room.

What will it cost?

Pricing comes with launch. The goal is a fraction of what a human coach charges.
